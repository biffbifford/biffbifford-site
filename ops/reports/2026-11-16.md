### 1. Tuesday, November 17, 9:30 am — The Carol Burnett Show

In the "Went with the Wind!" sketch, Scarlett comes down the stairs in a dress made from the curtains, with the curtain rod still through the shoulders. Bob Mackie built the rod into the costume and kept it from the rest of the cast, so the laugh would be real. Harvey Korman cannot hold it together. Carol lands the line about seeing it in the window and not being able to resist it.

This was suggested last week and never posted. It is the Tim Conway post in a gown: a show people can quote, a joke that was a real surprise in the room, and one person at the center. Open on the rod. The ear-tug story has already been told. The curtain-rod sketch is still unused.

### 2. Friday, November 20, 9:30 am — All in the Family

On February 19, 1972, Sammy Davis Jr. played himself. He had left a briefcase in Archie Bunker's cab and came to the house on Hauser Street to get it back. Archie wanted a picture. On the count of three, Sammy kissed him on the cheek. It became one of the biggest laughs the show ever got. The director and the writer have told the origin two ways: one says the kiss was added because the written ending felt weak, and one says the kiss was already in the draft. Tell the moment. Leave the credit line soft.

This was suggested twice and never posted. It is the Hogan's Heroes shape: a comedy moment that is also about something true, on a show this audience can quote. Friday is the best day for a normal post to be seen (23 posts, a typical one at about 4,000 views). Open on the kiss, then the briefcase.

### 3. Sunday, November 22, 9:30 am — The Betty White Show

In 1954, Betty White gave tap dancer Arthur Duncan his first job on television, on The Betty White Show. She later said stations in the South were going to take the show off the air if she got rid of him. Her answer, in her own words: "I'm sorry, but he stays. Live with it." Duncan said it was never a question, and he credits her with getting him started in show business.

This is the Mel Brooks shape, which just produced 1,482 likes and 296 reposts: a star people love, in their own words, doing the decent thing when it would have been easier not to. Betty White has not been posted. Open on her sentence. Sunday's typical post is already well liked (25 posts, a typical one with 62 likes), and Klinger just showed that a Sunday 9:30 story about one person can travel.
