Movie Puzzle

Spin the wheel, pick a letter, and solve the movie puzzle. Five puzzles a game.

How to play

  1. Spin the wheel. Pick a consonant to earn that dollar amount for every time it appears.
  2. Buy a vowel for $250 when you need one.
  3. When you know the answer, press Solve and type it in.
  4. You have six hearts. A letter that is not in the puzzle costs one. Bankrupt wipes out that puzzle's cash.
